Learn the Game

The old king is gone. As the head of one of four factions, accrue the most Influence over 4–6 Rounds (years) to claim the crown. Each Round flows through five phases; the Round guide walks you through them live at the table.

First game? Use the Setup tab's "First-game teaching setup", play a Short Game (4 Rounds), and treat Round 1 as learning, not winning.

The goal & the board

Most Influence in your Supply at game end wins. The board holds the Great Road (market of Kingdom Cards), the Map (Highlands, Plateau, Lowlands — each with two Locations), the Order Track and Round Track, plus the three Councils. You own a Player Board, a 14-card Basic Deck (identical across factions), a Herald, five Supporters, four Tactic tiles and a Site of Power with five buyable faction-unique cards.

Reading a card

Top left: Strength (0–11) for Bids and Clashes, with Archetype and Traits beneath. Right edge: Votes (Councils) and Lore (Journeying). Bold text = Commands. Circular border = optional Action; hexagonal border = mandatory Effect.

A year in one breath

Spring: secretly bid a card for Kingdom Cards, place your Herald, lay one face-down card at each Region, take Actions (e.g. place Supporters). Summer: resolve a Clash in each Region — reveal, trick, tally Strength; winners claim Location rewards. Autumn: Govern into Councils, Journey for Lore, buy from your Site of Power. Winter: cleanup. Then a new year begins, turn order reset by Influence.

Key habits

  • Don't fight everywhere — pick one priority Region per Round.
  • Guard your card economy — Rally cards home, keep your hand full, fear Attrition.
  • Track enemy Agents — until they're accounted for, shield your best cards from Deadly.
  • Recover your pieces — Supporters left on the Map are lost in Winter; the Shrine and Council of Oaths bring things back.
  • Lose with style — Flank away, Retreat, or trade eliminations; win by small margins.

Table Reference

Round sequence at a glance
  1. Start of the Year (skip in Round 1) — draw to Hand Size → reorder Order Track by Influence (ties reverse previous order).
  2. Spring — Place Bids → Resolve Bids (highest first: take / steal / return) → repopulate Great Road → Place Heralds → 1 face-down card next to each Region → Spring Action Step.
  3. Summer — last player places Clash Markers → per Region: Reveal → Day Actions → Night Effects → Tally → claim rewards.
  4. Autumn — turn order, free sequence: Govern ×1, Journey ×1, Actions/Commands.
  5. Winter — Winter Effects → cleanup → advance Round Track.
Commands
  • Ambush (Day, optional) — if this card is Active in the current Clash, add a face-down card from hand to the Clash. Once per Clash per card.
  • Retreat (Day, optional) — move any of your Active cards in this Region (incl. this one) to hand; also return your Herald and Supporters here to your board.
  • Flank (Day, optional) — move this card to a different unresolved Region. None left = no Flank.
  • Rally (Self / Any X) (listed step, optional) — return this card / up to X Active cards to hand.
  • Deploy (X) (listed step, optional) — play this card from hand face-up next to a Region with X Influence from the Reserve on it; the Influence keeps it Active through Winter.
  • Deadly (Night, mandatory) — Eliminates all opponents' Active cards in the current Clash (→ Lost Pile). Multiple Deadlies kill each other simultaneously.
Traits
  • Invulnerable — immune to Elimination (Deadly or any other source).
  • Resilient — when Eliminated, goes to your Discard Pile instead of the Lost Pile.
  • Pathfinder — when used to Journey, goes to your Discard Pile instead of the Lost Pile.

Map: Regions & Location Rewards

Highlands

  • Castle — Gain 1 Influence & Govern with one card that is Active or in your hand. If you do, discard all other cards at the chosen Council.
  • Wilderness — Gain 1 Influence & Journey with one card that is Active or in your hand.

Plateau

  • Harvest Field — Gain 1 Influence & take the Kingdom's Favour (or reset yours to III).
  • Battlefield — Gain 2 Influence.

Lowlands

  • Shrine — Gain 1 Influence & move up to three cards that are Active and/or in your hand to the bottom of your Deck, in any order.
  • Necropolis — Gain 1 Influence & shuffle your Discard Pile and draw up to three cards; you may discard any number of cards from your hand.
Winner picks ONE Location in the Region. Herald bonus first (1 Influence + steal 1 from each rival Herald there), then the printed reward. Printed Influence is mandatory; extra text optional. Neglecting the Lowlands starves your card economy.
Councils
  • Council of Relics — when resolving your Herald Reward, for each of your Votes you discard from here, gain 1 Influence.
  • Council of Secrets — Autumn Action: place Faction Markers on Locations equal to your Votes here. A Location with 4+ of your markers: remove them and claim its Location Reward, ignoring all Influence on it. Markers persist between Rounds.
  • Council of Oaths — Autumn Action: per Vote here, move one Supporter from Map/Lost Pile to your Player Board; +1 extra with the most Votes here.
  • Cards removed from a Council go to their owner's Discard Pile. The Castle reward clears the chosen Council.
Bidding & Kingdom Cards
  • Only the Bid card's Strength counts (plus Bid-specific bonuses). Ties resolve in Order Track order.
  • Options: take from Great Road / steal (Bid must exceed the Occupying card's Strength) / return card to hand.
  • Stolen from? Your Occupying card returns to your hand.
  • Max two Occupied Kingdom Cards; replacing one discards it and returns its occupier to hand.
  • Repopulate: discard the rightmost leftover (two if nobody bought), slide right, refill, flip the deck's top card.
  • Bold text on an acquired Kingdom Card is mandatory on acquisition.
Hand, Deck & Attrition
  • Attrition: drawing from an empty deck → shuffle Discard Pile into a new deck, Hand Size −1 (min 3), draw to new Hand Size. Lost Pile cards do NOT return.
  • Hand Size: max 8, min 3. Over limit → discard down. Cards gained over limit go on top of your deck.
  • A fuller hand at Round end = fewer draws = less Attrition risk.
Kingdom's Favour

Sits on the Harvest Field. Claiming the Harvest Field reward takes it (or resets yours to III). Grants a faction-specific Action; each use rotates it down. The final use returns it to the Harvest Field. First end-game tiebreaker.

Card zones & information
  • Active = face-up next to a Region. Nothing else is Active.
  • Discard Pile (public) reshuffles on Attrition; Lost Pile (public, shared) does not. Burned cards leave the game.
  • Private: your hand and your face-down cards. Hidden to all: decks. Everything face-up is public.
  • Card text beats rulebook. "Printed" ≠ "gained"; blanked information is ignored.
Game end & tiebreakers
  1. If your Site of Power is empty: 1 Influence per 2 Lore in Supply.
  2. Most Influence in Supply wins.
  3. Tie → Kingdom's Favour holder; else highest on the Order Track.
Common mistakes
  • Forgetting to discard the rightmost Great Road card before refilling (two if nobody bought).
  • Deployed cards need Influence on them to survive Winter cleanup.
  • Supporters left on the Map in Winter go to the Lost Pile, not home.
  • A tie-triggered Clash is a fresh Clash — earlier cards, Supporters and abilities don't carry over.
  • Night Effects (incl. Deadly) are mandatory and simultaneous.
  • Lore can only be spent at the moment you gain Lore.
  • Autumn's free Govern/Journey must use cards from hand.

Keywords A–Z

    Factions

    All factions share the same 14-card Basic Deck. Their identity comes from four unique Tactics, five buyable Site of Power cards and a faction Kingdom's Favour Action. Full card texts & images: Cards tab.

    The Nobility

    Defence, control, locking down what is theirs. The old order, stirring awake.

    Tactics

    • Martial Law (2 uses) — lock down a Region with an Active War Machine.
    • School of the Stones — Supporters +1 Strength this Round and stay on the Map in Winter.
    • Witch Trial — clear opponents from one Council; gain Lore moving your Council members to your deck.
    • Royal Seal — Bid: set all opponents' bidding Strength to 0.

    Kingdom's Favour — Regal Guard

    Choose one: gain 1 Influence, or your Heir gains Invulnerable for the rest of the Round.

    Site of Power highlights

    • The Viceroy (Champion, Str 11) · The Queen, Long Ago (Heir) · The High Court · Gilded Wall (War Machine) · Royal Bank (HQ: cards without printed Votes gain +1 Vote).

    First game kit: Champion, Gilded Wall, Royal Bank. Starter Tactic: School of the Stones.

    The Clans

    Mobility, momentum, overwhelming force. Exiles sailing home.

    Tactics

    • School of the Storms — place the Clash Markers this Round; gain Lore for most Supporters, Influence for most faction cards.
    • Sea Lanes (3 uses) — Heir/Captain/War Machine gains Flank until end of Round.
    • River Runners — steal an Occupied Kingdom Card.
    • Salt Toughness — move Active/discarded cards to hand.

    Kingdom's Favour — Prophet of the Waves

    Move your Herald to a Location in the current Region; move up to two Supporters from other Regions here.

    Site of Power highlights

    • Raid & Raze · New Havens Harbour (HQ: your cards gain Resilient; recover Supporters) · Wandering Embassy (HQ: Traders gain Flank).

    First game kit: Champion, Raid & Raze, New Havens Harbour. Starter Tactic: Salt Toughness.

    The Gathering

    Card manipulation, subversion, creeping accrual of power. The cult that grows each night.

    Tactics

    • Moonlit Foragers — acquire a discarded Kingdom Card.
    • Out Beyond the Lights — swap two of your Active cards.
    • School of the Stars — replace two Supporters with a Lost Pile card.
    • Ritual Sacrifice — gain Lore moving Active/discarded cards to the Lost Pile; recover one discard.

    Kingdom's Favour — Herald of the Moon

    Move one of your Active cards in your Herald's Region to your hand.

    Site of Power highlights

    • Ritual of the Eye (lowest Strength above 0 wins the Clash — the Champion-killer) · The Door in the Night (HQ: grant Ambush, even from your Discard Pile).

    First game kit: Champion, Ritual of the Eye, Moonshine Cove. Starter Tactic: Out Beyond the Lights.

    The Uprising

    Scheming, feints, eliminating rival cards. Generations of grief bared like teeth.

    Tactics

    • False Orders — swap two face-down faction cards, once per opponent.
    • Night Raids — opponents discard one card per Kingdom Card you Occupy.
    • School of the Streets — your Heir gains Deadly; your Deadly eliminations grant +1 Influence and +1 Lore.
    • Safehouses (3 uses) — a faction card gains Retreat.

    Kingdom's Favour — Spy Network

    Look at all face-down cards in a Region; you may swap one of your face-down cards in any Region with an Occupying card or a card from hand.

    Site of Power highlights

    • Black Powder Ploy · Moonshine Cove (HQ: extra Kingdom Card space) · The Syndicate (HQ: Votes from acquiring Kingdom Cards).

    First game kit: Champion, Black Powder Ploy, The Door in the Night. Starter Tactic: School of the Streets.

    Setup

    Choose game length first: Standard 5 Rounds (recommended), Short 4, Extended 6.

    • Kingdom Board central, Councils Tile above. Season Marker on Spring-1, Round Marker on 1.
    • Shuffle 51 Kingdom Cards → deck on the Great Road's left space. Fill three spaces face-up, flip the deck's top card (4 face-up).
    • Reserve: all Influence and Lore tokens beside the board.
    • Kingdom's Favour disc on the Harvest Field (III up).
    • Clash Markers stacked (III bottom, I top) in their slot.
    • Each player: faction, Player Board, Herald + 5 Supporters in their slots.
    • Hand Size Marker top-left, starting size aligned to the arrow.
    • Four Tactic tiles text-side up; follow printed SETUP text.
    • Faction Markers in a pile; Site of Power tile beside your board.
    • Pull the 5 Lore-cost cards (Advanced + HQ) from your deck onto your Site of Power.
    • Set your Heir aside, shuffle the rest as your Deck. Heir to hand, draw to 6.
    • Order Track: oldest first (or random). First player takes the Active Player Marker.

    From the official Teaching Guide — use together for a guided first Round. The teacher goes first on the Order Track.

    • Play a Short Game (4 Rounds).
    • Build the 30-card teaching Kingdom Deck (Teaching Guide p.3); rest back in the box.
    • Stack the 8 scripted cards on top, "Remote Bothies" uppermost: Ruined Citadel, Smugglers' Pass, Old King's Helm, Splendid Guard, Mustering Banner, Unearthed Iron Gear, The Sword That Slept, Remote Bothies.
    • Sites of Power: 3 cards each — Champion plus: Nobility Gilded Wall + Royal Bank · Clans Raid & Raze + New Havens Harbour · Uprising Black Powder Ploy + The Door in the Night · Gathering Ritual of the Eye + Moonshine Cove.
    • Tactics: all Exhausted, or one starter Tactic each — Nobility School of the Stones · Clans Salt Toughness · Gathering Out Beyond the Lights · Uprising School of the Streets.
    • Teacher first on the Order Track.
    • Round 1: Clash order I Highlands, II Plateau, III Lowlands. Teach stealing Kingdom Cards in Round 2.
    • After Round 1: group reflection — continue or restart.

    For experienced groups; any game length.

    • After board setup, deal each player 5 face-down Kingdom Cards (look at your own; don't show).
    • After drawing hands, one Mulligan each: return non-starting-icon cards to the top of the deck, shuffle, redraw.
    • Choose 1 of your 5 Kingdom Cards face-down onto your board with a hand card on top. Shuffle the rest into the Kingdom Deck.
    • After turn order: reveal all — the Faction Card Occupies the Kingdom Card. Resolve bold text in turn order. Draw to Hand Size.
